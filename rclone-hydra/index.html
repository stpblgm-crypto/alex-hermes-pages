rclone-hydra

A personal, owner-operated connection that uses rclone and Google Drive to back up and restore the owner's files. It is not a Google product and does not offer public account registration.

Purpose and access

The connection lists files and folders, uploads owner-selected files and backup archives, and downloads them for integrity checks or restoration. Operations are performed for the owner's authorized tasks.

Google OAuth consent determines the technical access granted to the installation. Selecting a destination folder in rclone is an operational setting, not an OAuth permission boundary. This site does not claim that Google access is limited to a single folder.

Backup handling

Restic backup repositories are encrypted before upload. Other files, including exported archives, are not automatically Restic-encrypted; protection depends on the particular transfer. Integrity checks and restoration checks must be completed before a backup is treated as verified.

Personal use and this website

This site contains documentation only. It has no Google sign-in, OAuth callback, file upload form or access to the owner's Google account. The connection runs separately in an owner-controlled installation. Publishing documentation does not change Google OAuth settings and does not establish Google verification or endorsement.

See the privacy policy for data handling, retention and revocation.
