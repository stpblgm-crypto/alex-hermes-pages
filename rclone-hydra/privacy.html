Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026. This policy covers the personal rclone-hydra Google Drive backup and restore connection and these documentation pages.

Data accessed and purpose

For owner-authorized backup, verification and restore tasks, the connection processes file and folder metadata, such as names, identifiers and sizes, and the contents of selected files. It lists Google Drive folders, uploads selected owner files or backup archives, and downloads files for verification or restoration. OAuth authorization information is used by the private installation to authenticate these operations.

Operational selection of files or a destination folder does not narrow the technical OAuth grant. The permission granted is the one shown during Google consent; this policy does not claim folder-limited OAuth access.

Storage and protection

Selected files and backup objects are stored in the owner's Google Drive and may also be held in owner-controlled local or server storage for backup, verification or recovery. Restic repositories are encrypted before upload. Other transfers, including exported archives, are not necessarily Restic-encrypted. Operational records may contain file metadata and verification results.

Credentials and authorization tokens belong in the private installation with restricted access, not in these public pages or their repository. Backup contents and Google account data are not published to this documentation site.

Use and sharing

The connection's purpose is the owner's backup and recovery work. Google Drive receives the files selected for upload. Google user data must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve generalized or foundation AI models. This backup workflow does not require sending file contents to an AI provider; any separate transfer to another service requires the owner's authorization and review of that service's data-handling terms.

rclone-hydra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

The owner controls retention of backups, downloaded copies and operational records. No universal automatic deletion period is promised. Copies remain until removed under the owner's instructions; revoking access does not itself delete existing local, server or Drive copies.

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. This prevents future authorized access through that grant. For retention or deletion requests, contact stp.blgm@gmail.com without including passwords, tokens or private archive contents.

Website hosting

These static pages have no analytics scripts, forms, Google-account integration or OAuth callback handling. GitHub provides hosting and may process visitor information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
